3. Publish a current/past **UTC** date and inspect homepage headline selection (Featured first, then date, at most four). A future date must produce a truthful error. Cards have no dead article link; full public pages still need approved design.
4. Edit and save a revision without publishing. The private working preview changes, while homepage/**Published preview** retains the prior snapshot. Publish the revision and verify it becomes eligible for selection.
5. Unpublish: the card leaves the feed; emptying the collection hides News without restoring old fixtures. Verify Editor can manage articles and a real Group Lead/anonymous visitor cannot open News administration or APIs. No deletion or scheduled publishing control is supplied.
