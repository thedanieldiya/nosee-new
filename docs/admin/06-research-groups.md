## Current implementation

Super Admin manages `/admin/research`, `/new`, `/[groupId]`, `/[groupId]/edit` and `/[groupId]/members`. Group Leads are redirected from the research root into their single assigned group, and may edit, preview, publish/unpublish and manage its participants immediately. Editor has no research access. Pages, service queries, APIs and autocomplete recheck server authority; no role switcher, public signup or scope inferred from email exists.

Group fields: name, unique lowercase URL slug, short introduction, plain-text full description, up to eight repeatable objectives/focus areas, cover image/alt and Draft/Published state. Content, roles, status and record versions are strictly validated. Saves update a **private working copy**. Explicit Publish validates complete content and records a separate immutable-at-that-instant published snapshot; later saves cannot silently change it. Unpublish removes eligibility for published reads, preserving the content/history. No group deletion.

A private **Preview** at `/admin/research/[groupId]?preview=1` shows the saved working copy in the admin workspace, with current role/scope checks and `private, no-store` responses. It is not an approved public-page design or a shareable draft token. Publish/unpublish request revalidation of `/research` and old/new public slugs after the transaction. Cache refresh failure reports persisted state with refresh unconfirmed, rather than claiming rollback.

Members tab uses keyboard-operable, debounced, cancellable authenticated POST autocomplete. Results contain only **ID, first/last name, affiliation and optimistic version**; no email, phone, notes, answers, interests, member-detail link or application lookup. Only Active, nondeleted members may be added. There is no person-creation/free-text fallback. Current association uniqueness prevents duplicates; title/order edits and removal affect this group only. Removed associations remain historical. The private organisation Member detail shows participant and Lead-assignment history. Group membership never publishes private data or creates a public leadership profile.

## Identity and lifecycle

Only Super Admin may assign/replace/remove Leads. Select an existing active administrator **and** an Active nondeleted member, explicitly confirm the identity, and submit current account/member/group versions. Identity is persisted by stable IDs; matching names/emails never establish it automatically. A person/account can lead one group at a time; conflicting assignments require explicit removal. Assignment changes the selected account to Group Lead, revokes old sessions/access versions and preserves self/last-Super-Admin safeguards. Removing/replacing scope leaves the separate account, password and active flag intact. No automatic demotion to Editor, which would grant unrelated publishing rights.

A02's deferral is lifted **only through real research-group context**. Settings → Administrators directs scoped assignment/invitation into Research; its generic role form does not permit an unscoped Lead. An unassigned/ineligible Lead account is retained but cannot sign in or use protected routes until explicitly reassigned or changed to another eligible role by Super Admin. The directory distinguishes account active state from usable research access.

To invite a member without an administrator account, select that exact member in the group and confirm. Existing transport/template/code-hash/48-hour expiry/revoke/resend/password and one-time consumption are reused. The recipient is the selected member's current stored email, not a supplied alternative. Invitations persist real group/member IDs and member version, reserve the group and identity, and recheck eligibility/version/email at redemption under the authority lock. Changed identities require revoke/new reviewed invitation; explicit resend rotates the credential but does not bypass eligibility. A current Lead must be removed before inviting its replacement. No manual-code distribution or sender/config bypass. Sending is unavailable without existing approved Resend configuration; provider acceptance never means inbox delivery. Membership revocation while a request is in flight invalidates activation and is reported honestly even if the provider accepted its email.

The shared PostgreSQL advisory lock **4631002** serializes research mutations with member status/deletion, administrator role/access and invitation issue/redemption. Every research transaction rechecks current active administrator/access version and, for Leads, a real nonrevoked assignment to an Active nondeleted member in the exact group. Sign-in/session reads check the same eligibility. Invitation expiry uses current database wall-clock time after lock waits.

Member deactivation/deletion atomically removes current participation, revokes Lead assignments/sessions, invalidates pending Lead credentials and appends safe audits with the membership change. Separate administrator accounts and published profile content remain. Reactivation does **not** restore removed participation or revoked assignments. Re-adding an email creates a different member ID with no inherited group/Lead privileges. Removed/revoked associations, source applications/decisions and audit history are retained; this is not personal-data erasure.

## Public rendering and image dependency

**Confirmed source gap:** pinned Laravel `9872c4e` has no `/research` GET routes or research Blade templates. `content/research/index.php` contains only a starter introduction; `areas/climate-science.php` is a starter record, while four other navigation areas lack records. The Next app has no research pages. Its homepage Research Outputs are **publications**, not group pages. [Source map](../migration-map.md#2-actual-route-inventory).

Accordingly **no new public layout, member roster, scientific copy or content import was invented**. `/research` and its existing area links retain their existing 404 behavior, including after database publication. `src/lib/research/public.ts` provides a validated **published-snapshot-only** read contract, excluding all identities/rosters/working drafts, ready for the separately approved P4 renderer. Public landing/detail layout and any mapping of existing navigation-area slugs to groups require owner approval; publication is database-ready, **not visible on a newly implemented public page**. Existing public content/pages are untouched.

**Storage:** no durable upload service/credentials or media-record system exists. The editor can persist a cover selected from four existing repository/deployment assets plus owner-entered alt text; arbitrary URL/data/SVG/path inputs are rejected and no files are written. These selections survive deployment, unlike local temporary previews. New file uploads are unavailable. To enable them later, the owner must approve a durable object-storage provider/bucket, private server-write credentials and allowed delivery origin, followed by its contextual upload adapter, byte/type/size validation and durable ownership/metadata integration. Environment variables depend on that selected provider; no fictitious configuration switch, purchase, provider connection or ephemeral upload is presented as working. No existing image is reassigned to public group content automatically.
